Interactive disease model

Deterministic SIRS Dashboard

Explore a closed population of 10,000 people moving between susceptible, infected, recovered, and back to susceptible compartments in 1-day timesteps.

Model

SIRS equations

dS/dt = -βSI/N + ωR

dI/dt = βSI/N - γI

dR/dt = γI - ωR

β
contact rate × infection probability
γ
1 / infectious period
ω
1 / immunity duration
N
10,000

Parameters

Output

Population over time
